SCENIC AUTHORING GUIDELINES (CARLA DOMAIN)

The following conventions constrain how a Scenic scenario script must be written
for the CARLA simulator. Adhere to them exactly. 


1. WORLD MODEL AND IMPORTS
   - Declare the world model as, and only as:
         model scenic.simulators.carla.model
   - Do not add any other import or model statement.


2. CONSTRUCTS THAT DO NOT EXIST (NEVER USE)
   - CrossingAction(...) and CrossRoadAction()      -- not defined.
   - Vector2                                        -- not a type.
   - The `pass` statement                           -- not supported.
   - `wait <X> seconds`                             -- invalid syntax; see section 7.
   - The specifiers "along left" and "along right"  -- not valid.
   - Attribute Lane.forwardDir                      -- does not exist.
   - Attribute Lane.orientation                     -- do not use; use roadDirection (section 4).


3. ACTORS
   - The only classes available to instantiate actors are:

         ATM, Advertisement, Barrel, Barrier, Bench, Bicycle, Box, Bus, BusStop,
         Car, CarlaActor, Case, Chair, Cone, Container, CreasedBox, Debris,
         Garbage, Gnome, IronPlate, Kiosk, Mailbox, Motorcycle, NPCCar,
         Pedestrian, PlantPot, Prop, Table, TrafficWarning, Trash, Truck, Van,
         Vehicle, VendingMachine

   - Available blueprints are listed in blueprints.scenic; available actor
     types are listed in models.scenic.
   - The `with velocity` specifier is not available when defining an actor.


4. POSITIONING, HEADINGS, AND roadDirection
   - roadDirection is a built-in vector field. Never assign to it and never
     redefine it as a variable.
   - After a `following` expression, do not read EgoSpawnPt.heading or
     lane.centerline; refer to roadDirection instead. For example:

         CrossingPt = OrientedPoint following roadDirection from EgoSpawnPt
                      for OPT_PED_CROSSING_DISTANCE


5. OFFSETS AND THE SHIFT CONVENTION
   - Lateral sign convention: a negative X offset is to the left, a positive X
     offset is to the right.
   - Define a shift as `SHIFT = <x> @ <y>` and apply it with
     `<point> offset along <heading> by SHIFT`, where <heading> must be a
     heading or a vector field.
   - The following are all invalid ways to offset a point or an actor:

         EgoSpawnPt.offset(along_lane=-10)
         EgoSpawnPt.offsetBy(...)
         egoTrajectory.offset(shift1)
         EgoSpawnPt.goAlong(egoTrajectory, d) + OrientedPoint.offset(...)

   - Spawn points expose no positioning helper methods, including .offset(),
     .offsetAlongRightBy(), .goingForward(), and .offset_by().


6. SPAWNING ACTORS
   - Use only the spawning forms shown in the examples.
   - Canonical form:

         Actor = Pedestrian at <point> offset along <heading> by SHIFT

   - Pedestrian, offset from the end-lane point (e.g. to appear after a turn):

         SHIFT = OPT_GEO_X_DISTANCE @ OPT_GEO_Y_DISTANCE
         AdvAgent = Pedestrian at endLanePt offset along endLanePt.heading by SHIFT

   - Pedestrian, full specification with a crossing behavior:

         pedLtoR = Pedestrian at crossingPt offset along roadDirection
                       by (OPT_PED_LEFT_OFFSET, OPT_PED_LEFT_DIST),
                   with heading crossingPt.heading - 90 deg,
                   with regionContainedIn None,
                   with behavior LeftToRightCrossing()


7. BEHAVIORS
   - Express an idle / blocking behavior through a dedicated behavior; never
     write `wait <X> seconds` directly:

         behavior WaitBehavior():
             wait

         behavior OtherCarBehavior():
             do WaitBehavior() for 10 seconds

   - To make the ego change into its current lane:

         do LaneChangeBehavior(network.laneSectionAt(ego))

   - LaneChangeBehavior takes a LaneSection argument, not a Lane.


8. LANES VERSUS LANE SECTIONS
   - Do not index the lane collection (network.lanes[0]) and do not compare
     lanes with == or != (for example, `network.lane != ego_lane` is invalid).
   - _laneToLeft and _laneToRight are attributes of a LaneSection, not of a Lane.
   - When several vehicles are involved, operate on lane sections rather than
     lanes.
   - To select a lane section whose left neighbor runs in the opposite
     direction:

         laneSecsWithLeftLane = []
         for lane in network.lanes:
             for laneSec in lane.sections:
                 if laneSec._laneToLeft is not None:
                     if laneSec._laneToLeft.isForward is not laneSec.isForward:
                         laneSecsWithLeftLane.append(laneSec)


9. TRAJECTORIES
   - A trajectory is an ordered list of lanes, from start lane through the
     connecting lane to the end lane:

         [ego_maneuver.startLane, ego_maneuver.connectingLane, ego_maneuver.endLane]


10. RECIPES
   - Parking spots (shoulder regions that have an adjacent sidewalk):

         parkingSpots = []
         for lane in network.lanes:
             laneGroup = lane.group
             if laneGroup._shoulder is not None and laneGroup._sidewalk is not None:
                 parkingSpots.append(laneGroup.shoulder)
         lane = Uniform(*parkingSpots)
